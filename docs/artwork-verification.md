# Artwork verification notes

Research date: November 13, 2026. These are visual research notes, not a final game script. The five-work virtual exhibition is approved; implementation design is pending approval.

## The Potato Eaters — exact MoMA lithograph

Record: https://www.moma.org/collection/works/64997

Reference image opened from that record and visually inspected:
https://www.moma.org/media/W1siZiIsIjEzMzcxOCJdLFsicCIsImNvbnZlcnQiLCItcXVhbGl0eSA5MCAtcmVzaXplIDIwMDB4MjAwMFx1MDAzZSJdXQ.jpg?sha=5bef61abceba6890

MoMA identifies this as an 1885 lithograph, object 239.1963, not on view at the check. The reference shows five seated figures around a table. One foreground figure faces away from the viewer. A lamp hangs above the group. A figure at the left pours from a pot. There is a small framed picture at the upper right of the composition.

Safe visual-question anchors: number of figures (five); light source (hanging lamp); orientation of the foreground figure (back toward viewer); position of the pouring pot (left side). Avoid copying left/right assumptions from the oil painting, which is a different object. Do not infer the figures' exact ages, relationships, or feelings as factual answers.

## Portrait of Dr. Gachet (Man with a Pipe) — exact MoMA etching

Record: https://www.moma.org/collection/works/71621

Reference image opened from that record and visually inspected:
https://www.moma.org/media/W1siZiIsIjIxMjIyOSJdLFsicCIsImNvbnZlcnQiLCItcXVhbGl0eSA5MCAtcmVzaXplIDIwMDB4MjAwMFx1MDAzZSJdXQ.jpg?sha=1779392f5320dd24

MoMA identifies this as an 1890 etching, object 594.1940, not on view at the check. The reference shows a single man with a pipe held near his face. His raised hand appears on the viewer's left. Curved lines describe the hair; closely spaced and crossing lines darken the jacket. The printed composition occupies only part of a larger pale sheet.

Safe visual-question anchors: object held (pipe); how darker jacket areas are made (many closely spaced/crossing lines); number of sitters (one); hair marks (curving lines). Do not substitute a painted portrait of Gachet, or ask about colors from a painted version.

## Asset handling

The downloaded research references reside in temporary storage, not the deployable app. Image source, reproduction choice, and attribution must be documented when assets are selected. Keep the actual composition large enough to inspect on mobile; a whole-sheet thumbnail alone would make the Gachet details difficult to see. Any detail view must be identified and preserve access to the full work.
