# Artwork sources

All five images were downloaded from the image link in the corresponding MoMA collection record and visually inspected on November 13, 2026. They depict works by Vincent van Gogh (1853–1890). Original artworks are public domain; these reference reproductions are supplied by MoMA. No images are AI generated. MoMA links and credit lines remain accessible in the app. This is an independent educational prototype, not a MoMA product; consult MoMA's reproduction guidance before wider institutional distribution.

Visual verification: Starry Night shows a dark cypress at left, a crescent moon at upper right, a village and pointed steeple beneath the sky, and curved painted bands. Olive Trees shows twisting trunks, a pale cloud, mountains, and a landscape without painted people. Roulin shows POSTES on a blue cap, a curling beard, blue clothing and a patterned floral background. The Potato Eaters lithograph shows five seated figures, a hanging lamp, a foreground figure facing away and a pouring pot at left. The Gachet etching shows a pipe, curved hair and crossing lines in the jacket. All answer keys are anchored to these visible details; emotions and themes are framed as interpretation.

The app preserves full images with object-fit: contain. Enlarged views allow inspection of the prints without substituting a different version. MoMA's Potato Eaters is a lithograph and Gachet is an etching, not the similarly named paintings.
